A third place, found by happy accident.

Our owner had a history degree from OU, a head full of travels across Europe, and no plans to run a pub. He came into the business almost by accident, and what he found wasn’t just a restaurant. It was a clubhouse.

Some regulars come in five or six days a week. They know the bartenders by name, they have a usual, and they’ll happily tell you what to order. We like to think the room has that effect on people.

Tucked behind the greenery in an old house on the corner of Boyd and Chautauqua, The Library has been named one of America’s 10 Best College Town Bars by Esquire. So come as you are. You don’t have to cook tonight; that’s our job.

— The Library family, part of GL Dining

Library Rules

The fine print, posted by the door.

  1. Leave the pets at home. Service animals are always welcome.
  2. Decorate the reading rooms all you like, but please, no confetti or glitter.
  3. Outside desserts are welcome if they come from a commercial bakery.
  4. Need a projector for a presentation? Bring your own and we’ll find you an outlet.
  5. Catering orders are taken by phone, not online.
  6. You must be 21 or older to order from the bar.
